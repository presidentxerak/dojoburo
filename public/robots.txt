# DojoBuro · robots policy
#
# Requested: "Vérifie la cyber sécurité et l'anti hacking et scraping de
# données. Trouve une stratégie pour améliorer le SEO dans l'app".
#
# Two goals pull in opposite directions, so the policy draws one line:
#   · SEARCH is welcome: classic search engines AND the AI answer engines that
#     cite and link back (ChatGPT search, Perplexity, Claude search), because
#     that is where people now look for a course;
#   · TRAINING and bulk scraping are not: the crawlers that copy pages to train
#     models or resell data are refused.
# Private pages (profile, payment return, private messages, member pages) are
# never indexed. robots.txt only guides the polite: the API also refuses
# self-declared scrapers and rate-limits reads (api/_lib/scrapers.ts).
